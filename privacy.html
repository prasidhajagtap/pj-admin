Who we are

PJ is an app developed and operated by Prasidha Jagtap, the data controller. Contact: prasidhaj0808@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • your Google account ID, email address and whether it is verified;
  • your name, first name and profile photo link;
  • the privacy notice version you accepted and when;
  • when your account was created and when you last signed in;
  • which version (build) of the app you last used, so we can tell you when an update is required;
  • if an administrator suspends your account, when and why.

When you use friends and Bluetooth play, we also store:

  • your friends and friend requests (who asked whom, and when);
  • nudges you send and receive, and when they were delivered;
  • games you host or are invited to, and whether you joined;
  • for each Bluetooth game session: a random session ID, your phone model, Android version, app build, and a technical log of the connection (for example: found the host, connected, verified, disconnected, Bluetooth turned off, and game results);
  • if you allow notifications: a push notification token for each phone you are signed in on, so nudges, game invites and announcements reach you when PJ is closed. It is deleted when you sign out.

Whether you are online is kept only in the server's memory for about 30 seconds and is never stored. On the Add user screen your phone shows your first name to nearby phones over Bluetooth, with a random code that changes every few minutes; only signed-in PJ users can turn that code into your name and photo. Android asks for the "Nearby devices" (and, on Android 12 and older, location) permission to find nearby phones. PJ never reads or stores your location.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app: sign you in and show your name and photo. Basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Friends, nudges and games: let you find friends nearby, nudge them and play together. Basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep it secure: rate limiting, verifying that only invited friends join a game, and an audit log of administrator actions (which includes administrators' IP addresses). Basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Troubleshooting: the Bluetooth session log helps fix connection problems. Basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it

  • Google: provides the sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications (it receives the notification text and your phone's push token).
  • Cloudflare: carries encrypted traffic between the app and the PJ server.
  • GitHub: hosts this page and the admin page; it receives no user data from the app.

Your data is stored in a MySQL database on the developer's server in India. Transfers to the providers above are protected by their standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Friends: until you or your friend removes the friendship, or one of you deletes the account.
  • Nudges: 7 days.
  • Push notification tokens: until you sign out or Firebase reports the token as no longer valid.
  • Games and Bluetooth session logs: 30 days.
  • Administrator audit log: 365 days, then automatically deleted.

Your rights

You can access, export, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time:

  • In the app, Your data shows everything we store about you (access and portability).
  • In the app, Delete account permanently erases your account and data (erasure).
  • Or email prasidhaj0808@gmail.com; we reply within one month.

You may also complain to your data protection supervisory authority.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Bluetooth game connections are encrypted, and both phones prove they belong to invited players before any game data is exchanged; every game message is signed. Sign-in tokens are verified with Google, sessions are signed and expire, the app keeps its session in encrypted device storage, and database access is limited and audited.

Changes

If this notice changes, the version above changes and the app asks you to accept the new version.
