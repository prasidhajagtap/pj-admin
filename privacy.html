Asteriq Privacy NoticeVersion 2026-10-14

Who we are

Asteriq is an astrology app for entertainment, developed and operated by Prasidha Jagtap, the data controller (GDPR) and Data Fiduciary (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact and grievance officer: prasidhaj0808@gmail.com.

Asteriq is meant for people aged 18 or older. It is for entertainment only and is not professional, medical, psychological or financial advice.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • your Google account ID, email address and whether it is verified;
  • your name, first name and profile photo link;
  • the privacy notice version you accepted and when;
  • when your account was created and when you last signed in;
  • which version (build) of the app you last used, so we can tell you when an update is required;
  • if an administrator suspends your account, when and why.

When you build your astrology profile (only after you tick the consent box), we store:

  • your quiz answers and the personality scores worked out from them;
  • your birth chart: the zodiac signs and degrees of the Sun, Moon, rising sign and planets. Your birth date, time and place are sent once to work out the chart and are not stored;
  • your generation label (Gen Z, 90s-born or other), which you confirm. Your birth year is not stored;
  • your gender, only if you choose to give it (you can choose "Prefer not to say");
  • how accurate you rated your profile (1–10 stars) and any traits you marked as "not me";
  • your answers to the optional question of the day;
  • "feels true / not really" feedback on readings and daily tips, used to fit future readings to you;
  • the days you opened your forecast and, if you answer the evening check-in, whether your day was good or not;
  • the last life area you read.

If you allow notifications, we also store a push notification token for each phone you are signed in on, so announcements reach you when Asteriq is closed. It is deleted when you sign out.

So the app works without a connection, your profile and the next three days of forecasts and readings are also kept in the app's private storage on your phone; they are deleted when you sign out or delete your account. The 9:45 am and 9:30 pm reminders, and the occasional nudge when you haven't opened Asteriq for 6 hours (at most two a day, never between 10 pm and 8 am, and you can turn nudges off in Me → Notifications), are planned on your phone itself from what it already has; we do not track when you see them. Today's good hours are worked out on your phone from the city you pick; that city stays on your phone and is never sent to us. If you add the Asteriq widget to your home screen, it shows your day from the same on-phone copy, and it goes blank when you sign out. Asteriq does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or Bluetooth. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Permissions on your phone

Asteriq asks for one permission, Notifications, explained on a "Before you start" screen first. It is optional: the app works the same without it, just without reminders. Withdraw it any time in the app (Me → Notifications, or About → Permissions) or in Android Settings → Apps → Asteriq. Withdrawing is as easy as allowing.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the app: sign you in and show your name and photo. Basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Your astrology profile, readings, forecast and check-ins: built only because you ask for them. Basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); for the evening "how was your day" answer, explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a); DPDP Act s. 6). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your astrology profile (Me → Delete my astrology profile) or your account.
  • Learning from your feedback: to choose readings that fit you better. Basis: your consent, as above.
  • To keep it secure: rate limiting and an audit log of administrator actions (which includes the administrator's IP address). Basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

No automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you.

Who processes it

  • Google: provides the sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications (it receives the notification text and your phone's push token).
  • Cloudflare: carries encrypted traffic between the app and the Asteriq server.
  • GitHub: hosts this page, the download page and the admin page; it receives no user data from the app.

Your data is stored in a MySQL database on the developer's server in India. Transfers to the providers above are protected by their standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Astrology profile (answers, chart, scores, rating, generation, gender, question-of-the-day answers): until you delete the profile or your account.
  • Reading and tip feedback: 180 days.
  • Days opened and evening check-ins: 90 days.
  • Push notification tokens: until you sign out or Firebase reports the token as no longer valid.
  • Administrator audit log: 365 days, then automatically deleted.

Data from earlier versions of the app (friends, nudges, games and Bluetooth logs) has been deleted.

Your rights

You can access, export, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time:

  • In the app, About → Your data shows everything we store about you (access and portability).
  • Me → Retake the quiz corrects your profile; Me → Delete my astrology profile erases it.
  • About → Delete account permanently erases your account and all data (erasure).
  • Or email prasidhaj0808@gmail.com; we reply within one month.

Under India's DPDP Act you also have the right to information about your data, to correction and erasure, to grievance redressal, and to nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act. Write to the grievance officer above; we reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. In the EU/UK you may complain to your data protection supervisory authority.

If you are going through a hard time

Asteriq is not a substitute for real help. In India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416): free, confidential and open 24×7.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are verified with Google, sessions are signed and expire, the app keeps its session in encrypted device storage, and database access is limited and audited. Only the owner's Google account can use the admin panel.

Changes

If this notice changes, the version above changes and the app asks you to accept the new version.
