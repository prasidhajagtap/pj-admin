PJ for Android

Add friends nearby, nudge them, and play together over Bluetooth with up to 4 phones.

Download PJ.apk

Android 7 or newer · about 50 MB · free

Install in three steps

  1. Tap Download PJ.apk on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (only for this install).
  3. Open PJ, sign in with Google, then use Add user next to your friend to find each other.

Updates arrive inside the app. iPhone isn't supported yet.
